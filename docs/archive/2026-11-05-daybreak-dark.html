FAQ

Common questions

What is Daybreak?+

Daybreak is the AI-powered CRM for retail by Data Does Not Lie. Connect Square or upload a CSV, and it turns your sales data into clear reports, daily plain-language recommendations, and email and SMS follow-up workflows you launch in one click.

What data sources does Daybreak support?+

Square connects out of the box, and any CSV export uploads in seconds with columns detected automatically. We can also connect almost any other system by API, like the SPOT point of sale for dry cleaners, for an additional setup charge.

Do I need an analyst to use Daybreak?+

No. Reports build themselves from your data, the AI explains what changed and why it matters in plain language, and every recommendation comes with a one-click action. It is built for owners, operators, and marketers, not data teams.

What does the AI actually do?+

Every day it reads your numbers, by store and channel, against your own history. When something deserves attention it tells you what changed, why it matters in dollars, and what to do about it, and it cites the data behind every number. Over MCP, an open standard for connecting AI to software, it can also build reports, workflows, smart lists, email templates, and SMS snippets on request.

Can Daybreak send email and SMS?+

Yes. Recommendations turn into live email and SMS workflows in one click: sequences with waits and skip rules, customers who come back drop out automatically, and opt-outs are handled on every text.

What does Daybreak cost?+

We walk through pricing on the demo call. Square and CSV setups are turnkey; connecting another system by API carries an additional setup charge.

Who is behind Daybreak?+

Data Does Not Lie, which also offers Custom Development (bespoke apps, dashboards, integrations, and AI assistants built on a company's own data) and Website Development: fast marketing sites with SEO and AEO built in, so search engines and AI assistants can find and quote your business.
