Questions

Pricing questions

Anything else? Ask us on a call.

How does the free month work?

On Growth and Premier you pay the one-time setup fee when you sign up (the Partner plan has none). Then you get 30 days with every feature. Your card is charged the monthly price when the 30 days end, and we email you a few days before.

Why is there a setup fee?

It covers getting you running: connecting your data, checking it came in right, and setting up your first lists and follow-up. On Premier we do all of it for you, including moving data from your old system. The Partner plan is self-serve, so it has no setup fee.

Is there a contract?

No. Every plan is month to month. Cancel from your billing settings and you keep read access for 30 days to export your data.

What if I have more stores than my plan includes?

Add stores on Growth or Premier for a monthly price per store. Past 20 stores, Enterprise is usually the better fit.

What happens when I run out of texts?

Included texts reset on the 1st. If you run out before then, buy a text pack, or turn on auto-recharge and we'll add one for you when you're running low. Unused pack texts never expire.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Move up or down as your business changes. Talk to us and we'll switch you over.

What if my point of sale isn't Square?

Upload a CSV export from any system and Daybreak matches the columns for you. Shopify is coming soon. For a direct connection to another database or point of sale, add a custom data connection.

What if a payment fails?

Everything keeps running for 7 days while you update your card, and Stripe retries the payment. After that the account becomes read-only until it's paid.

I'm an agency. Can I resell Daybreak?

Yes. The Partner plan lets you sell Daybreak under your own brand at your own prices. See the partner program.
