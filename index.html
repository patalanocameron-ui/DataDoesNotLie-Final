FAQ

Common questions

What is Daybreak?+

Daybreak is the AI CRM for retail stores, made by Data Does Not Lie. It turns your sales data into clear reports, a short list each morning of customers worth reaching and why, and email and text follow-up that runs itself.

Where does my data come from?+

Square connects directly and keeps syncing. Shopify is coming soon. Any other system can send a CSV export, and Daybreak matches the columns for you. We can also connect almost any database or point of sale by API for an added setup fee.

How much does it cost?+

Growth is $497 a month and Premier is $997 a month, each with a one-time setup fee and the first 30 days free. Enterprise starts at $2,500 a month. Agencies can resell Daybreak on the Partner plan. See the pricing page for everything included.

Do I need someone technical to use it?+

No. Reports build themselves, the advice is written in plain English, and every recommendation comes with a one-click action. It's built for owners and managers.

What does the AI actually do?+

Every morning it reads your numbers by store against your own history. When something deserves attention it tells you what changed, why it matters in dollars and what to do, and links to the customers or orders behind it. You can also chat with your data in Ask Daybreak, or connect Claude and other AI tools over MCP.

Can it send email and texts?+

Yes. Workflows send email and texts with waits and branches. Customers who come back drop out automatically, and unsubscribes and text opt-outs are honored on every send. Texting uses your own local number, registered with the carriers.

Can I cancel?+

Yes, any time. Plans are month to month. If you cancel you keep read access for 30 days to export your data.

Who is behind Daybreak?+

Data Does Not Lie, founded by Cameron Patalano. We also offer Custom Development and Website Development with SEO and AEO built in.
