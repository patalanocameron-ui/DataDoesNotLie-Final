Partner questions

When can I start?

Today. Sign up online, add your logo and colors, and create your first client. There is no setup fee, and the first 30 days are free.

Do my clients know Daybreak is behind it?

No. With white label, your clients see your name, logo and colors when they sign in and in the emails they receive.

Who supports my clients?

You do, as their main contact. We support you by email, with guides you can share.

How does the commission work?

When a client of yours buys an add-on directly, you earn 20% of what they pay for it, every month they keep it.

Can my clients text their customers?

Yes. Each client gets its own registered number. You buy text credits at wholesale and decide what to charge.

Can my clients pay me online?

Yes. Connect your own Stripe account, set the monthly price and an optional setup fee, and share your signup link. Clients pay you directly, and their account is created and the owner invited the moment they pay. If a client cancels, their account pauses. We take no cut of those payments.

Is there a minimum commitment?

No. The Partner plan is month to month, with no setup fee and 30 days free.
